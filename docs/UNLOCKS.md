# Unlocks: side jobs and recruiting the other heroes

What the game checks before it offers a side job, and before you can meet and recruit another of the five heroes in story mode. Read from the code on 2026-10-14 (functions named in `symbols/symbols.tsv`). **Tested so far: Gina's encounter** ([Test: meeting Gina](#test-meeting-gina-2026-10-14)); the jobs and the other heroes are untested. docs/SCRIPTS.md explains the mission system these build on.

## Terms used below

- **Story stage**: `g_StoryChapter` `0x0060BE10`, a counter from 1 to 11 that story events advance (several times per chapter, so it is not the "Chapter N" number on screen). Read it in the sandbox with `python tools/pcsx2.py r32 0x0060BE10`. V's save states: stage 8 (Aaron, at DOWNTOWN STREET, during Chapter 4: Miles says "It looks like a big drug deal is going down at the docks", the chapter 4 objective) and stage 6 (Lola, at LAS SOMBRAS CANTINA; that 2023 state no longer loads in PCSX2 2.x). So **stage 8 is Chapter 4**.
- **Criminal Level ranks**: the three scores of the CRIMINAL screen (docs/MECHANICS.md), 0-255 each: Leadership `0x0060D346`, Cash Flow `0x0060D348`, Charisma `0x0060D34A`. `Criminal_Rank` `0x003937C0` turns a score into a rank: 0-20 = 0, 21-60 = 1, **61-100 = 2**, 101-140 = 3, 141-190 = 4, 191-240 = 5, 241-255 = 6. The hero checks ask for **rank 2 or more, a score of at least 61**.
- **Locations**: `Location_Current` `0x002E7A50` and `Area_Current` `0x002FD630` return an id whose name is text 18000 + id (CDATA.XAF entry 386). All 54 are listed in [Location ids](#location-ids).
- **Game clears**: `g_ClearCount` `0x0060BADC`, how many times the game has been finished; the ending (`Ending_Task` `0x002AE7A0`) adds 1. Saved.
- **Jobs cleared %**: `Jobs_PercentCleared` `0x003A4A70` = jobs ever cleared / 50 (`g_JobCleared`). A new game from the title screen clears it. The new game started after the ending (`0x003D5860`, which keeps your money) keeps it and only resets this run's results (`g_JobResults`). So the 40% and 60% jobs of the early stages are meant for a second playthrough.

## Side jobs

### Where they are offered

The job menu (`JobMenu_Open` `0x0013FC50`) shows **list B when you talk to Melvin (person 106)** and **list A** at the other job brokers. `JobList_Build` `0x003A45B0` lists a job only when:

1. the story stage you are in now is one of its stages ("Stages" below); a job offered only in stage 6 can't be taken once the story moves on;
2. enough jobs have been cleared ("Jobs cleared");
3. the game has been finished enough times ("Game clears"; only job 45);
4. its special condition holds ("Extra");
5. you haven't completed it in this run; a failed job comes back;
6. no mission is running in slot 0 (finish or fail the current one first);
7. not in stage 7 while a story encounter is pending (`0x005FEBBC`), which blocks every job and event for that moment.

At most 34 jobs are listed at a time.

So **to see every job**: play stages 6 to 10 and take each job while its stage lasts. On a first playthrough the 40% and 60% jobs of stages 6 and 7 can't be reached (there aren't 20-30 jobs to clear yet); on a playthrough after the ending the cleared count carries over and they can. Job 45 (Friendly Abduction) also needs one game clear and a female hero.

## Recruiting the other heroes

In story mode the four heroes you aren't playing can be met and recruited. They are persons 22 (Lola), 53 (Jason G), 68 (Aaron), 107 (Raven) and 108 (Gina); person ids equal their template ids, and persons 153-157 are the heroes' copies used as the player. A recruited hero is an ally (person `+0x3A8` bit 0) and can join the party from the ally list.

**How it works.** Every time you change areas, the story code (`Story_AreaEvents` `0x0038CE00` and `Story_AreaEvents2` `0x0038B4A0`, switched by story stage) tries `Hero_StartEncounter(hero)` `0x003964F0` for that stage's heroes. It sets up a meeting at the hero's location when `Hero_CanMeet(hero)` `0x00398BA0` passes and the hero hasn't been met yet, and sets the hero's level to yours plus or minus 2 (`Hero_ScaleToPlayer` `0x0013EB30`). The normal after-knockout RECRUIT menu never applies to heroes (`Battle_CanTalkAfterKO` excludes persons 22, 53, 68, 107, 108, 110, 158 and 159), so recruiting happens in the hero's own encounter. What that encounter asks of you (a fight, a dialogue choice) wasn't traced yet.

### Test: meeting Gina (2026-10-14)

Tested by Claude in the sandbox PCSX2 (`tools/pcsx2.py`), watched by V, from V's save state 1 (Aaron, stage 8, Leadership 65, Cash Flow 171, Charisma 47):

1. Charisma set to 100 by memory (`pcsx2.py w16 0x0060D34A 100`), so both Charisma and Leadership were 61 or more.
2. Teleported to PLATFORM with the debug cheat `tools/debug/teleport.c` (`pcsx2.py w32 0x000F0000 18`). A street fight that had started first was finished; then the area change happened.
3. On arriving, Gina's encounter started at once: flags `0x5F6` and `0x1F41` set, encounter record `0x006C55C0` = `0x19` with event `0x34` at `0x006C55C6`, as read from the code.
4. It is a **cutscene**: Gina lies beaten up, Aaron asks "Are you OK?". During it her person `+0x3A8` became 3 (ally): **no fight and no negotiation**.
5. It ends with a menu: "Add Gina to the party" / "Keep on call".

Not tested: that she does *not* appear with Charisma below 61, and the other four heroes.

Within a stage the heroes are tried in this order and at most one encounter starts per area change: stage 7 Jason G; stage 8 Gina, Raven, Jason G; stage 9 Raven, Jason G; stage 10 Aaron, Raven, Jason G. Lola is checked on her own in stages 5-9.

**A changed look** (a disguise): `Appearance_UpdateFlags` `0x001BAC80` reads what you wear and look like (`g_Appearance` `0x0060BE50`: up to 38 parts, code = category x 100 + n; category names are text 14500 + category). In V's stage-8 save Aaron wears hat 1101 and flag `0x3FE` was set, which matches category 11 = HATS. Raven and Jason G need either:

- **brows other than your hero's own** (category 4; the heroes' own are 409 Lola, 408 Gina, 416 Aaron, 417 Raven, 418 Jason G); sets flag `0x3FF`; or
- **an item of category 10 and one of category 11 at the same time** (flags `0x3FD` and `0x3FE`). The game's category names say SOCKS and HATS; that pairing is odd enough to need a test.

Appearance item 2553 (category 25, the RIGHT LEG part slot) sets flag `0x4CE`, which blocks both.

**"Nobody assigned to the location"**: `0x001C4B80(location)` is true when one of the two people in the list at `0x0060D33E` / `0x0060D340` has that location in the per-person byte table at `0x0060D275` (filled by code near `0x001C3A00` that also sets story flags; probably allies sent to hold a place). Raven needs nobody at location 4, Jason G nobody at location 7.

**Flags set by the encounters** (for cheats and tests): Lola `0x1F3E` and `0x5F5`; Gina `0x1F41` and `0x5F6`; Raven `0x3C6`; Jason G `7999` and `0x3C8`; Aaron `0x1F42`. Flags `0x5F1` Raven, `0x5F2` Aaron, `0x5F4` Jason G, `0x5F5` Lola and `0x5F6` Gina mark a hero as met: while one is set, you're at their place and they aren't your ally yet, `Hero_CanMeet` stays true, which feeds the rumours (`Hero_HintState` `0x00398A60`).

### Test shortcuts (untested cheats)

- Criminal Level: write 255 to `0x0060D346`, `0x0060D348` and `0x0060D34A` (s16 each) to meet every rank requirement.
- Story stage: `0x0060BE10` (s16); changing it confuses story events, so only on a throwaway save.
- Recruit directly: set bits 0 and 1 of the hero's person `+0x3A8` (what `EXF_JOIN_FRIEND` does). Jason G is `g_Persons[53]`: `0x0060D360 + 53 x 0x43C + 0x3A8` = `0x0061B774` (byte).

Text 14532-14535 are ally skill names: PICKPOCKET, MARTIAL ARTIST, "Specialty:", CAR THIEF. `EXF_CHECK_DRESS` and `EXF_CHECK_DRESS_CATE` read the same list. ## Story chapter objectives

Text 31070-31090 (CDATA.XAF 386) are the on-screen objectives, three per chapter: Chapter 1 "Go to the bar called The Hole!", "Get the blacklist!", "Escape from the police station!"; Chapter 2 "Help Tracy!", "Decimate Fatima's Saints!", "Head for the basketball court!"; Chapter 3 "Get information from Tracy!", "Gather info around town!", "Save your allies from the assassin!"; Chapter 4 Tracy, "Get info on the drug deal from Eugene's men!", "Find Eugene and stop the drug deal!"; Chapter 5 Tracy, "Locate Carlo and get your hands on the bomb!", "Detonate the narcotics refinery!"; Chapter 6 Tracy, "Drop the hammer on Dr. Quincy!", "Head for the top floor of the Palm View Hotel!"; Chapter 7 "Go to the bar called The Hole!", "Head for the police station with Chester!", "Finish Wallace!". Matching them to story stages is open.
